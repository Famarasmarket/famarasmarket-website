Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to Famara'smarket.

1. Introduction

Famara'smarket respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

When you place an order, we may collect information needed to process and deliver your order, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery area and address
  • Your order information
  • Payment reference information when provided
  • Additional notes you choose to provide

3. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide may be used to:

  • Process your orders
  • Arrange delivery
  • Confirm payments where applicable
  • Communicate with you about your order
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve our shopping experience

4. Payment Information

Famara'smarket may provide payment instructions for available payment methods such as mobile money, bank payments and Cash on Delivery. Customers should only make payments using the payment information displayed through the official Famara'smarket checkout.

5. Order Information

Your order details may be used to identify and manage your order. Your Order ID should be kept safe because it may be used to check your order status.

6. Customer Reviews

If you submit a review, the information you choose to include may be displayed on the website as a customer review.

7. Local Storage

Some website features may use your browser's local storage to remember information such as shopping cart items, order information and reviews on your device.

8. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell your personal information. Information may be used or shared when necessary to process an order, arrange delivery, communicate with you or comply with applicable requirements.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used in connection with our services. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Third-Party Services

Our website may use external services such as WhatsApp or payment providers. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Your Choices

You may choose what information you provide when using the website. Some information is necessary for us to process and deliver an order.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, services or business practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Famara'smarket.
